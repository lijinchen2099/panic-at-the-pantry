You can remap gameplay keys under Options → Keyboard. Menu shortcuts remain fixed.

My keys have changed. How do I reset them?

Open Options → Keyboard and choose Reset Keys to restore the default bindings. When you assign a key that is already in use, the two actions swap keys.

How do I unlock Chef Pepperpot?

Defeat Baron von Toast, Duke de Blend, Madame Whistle, and Sir Sizzle on Spicy. Each boss counts once, in any order. The saved SPICY WINS checklist shows your progress. Cozy and Regular victories do not count toward this unlock. The Chef is always Spicy.

Why won’t some attacks break?

Crumb waves, bananas, and the Chef’s whisks are shielded. Their blue outline and shield icon identify them. Jump or dash through danger; bullets and energy balls do not destroy shielded attacks. Pink food can be parried.

How does the energy ball work?

Firing fills the five-card super meter. When it is full, press Q, or your remapped super key, to fire an energy ball in Sunny’s facing direction. It can miss, and it uses the full meter.

Why did the game pause?

The game pauses when it loses focus. Click back into the game and press Esc to resume. Opening controls help during a fight also pauses gameplay.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. Gameplay and saved progress are local to your Mac. The game does not include accounts, online play, ads, or in-app purchases.

What should I do if the game closes unexpectedly?

Reopen the game and try to reproduce the problem. If it happens again, report the game version, your Mac model and macOS version, and what you were doing. Share a screenshot if it helps, after removing private information.
